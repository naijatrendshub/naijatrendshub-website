How to revoke TikTok access

Step-by-step:

  1. Open TikTok app or go to TikTok Settings
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Security & Login → Manage third-party apps
  3. Find NaijaTrendsHub in the list
  4. Tap Remove access

After you revoke access, we will no longer be able to publish to your TikTok account, and your stored access tokens will be invalidated.

How to delete your data

We currently do not have an in-app "Delete Account" button.

To request deletion of your account and data:

  1. Send an email to naijatrendshub@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: DELETE MY DATA
  3. Include the email address or username associated with your account

We will verify your identity and delete your personal data and tokens (unless we must retain certain records for legal reasons). Typical processing time: 7 business days.
