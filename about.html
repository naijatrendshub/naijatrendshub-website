About NaijaTrendsHub

What we do

NaijaTrendsHub is an AI-powered content automation platform designed to help creators and media organizations publish trending Nigerian news, sports, and entertainment across multiple social media platforms.

How it works

Users connect their social media accounts via official platform authorization (OAuth). Our system then:

  • Analyzes trending Nigerian content from verified news sources
  • Generates platform-optimized captions, scripts, and media
  • Publishes content to your connected accounts (with your authorization)

Platforms we support

We integrate with TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Facebook, Telegram, Snapchat, Twitch, Farcaster, and Bluesky.

TikTok integration (what we use)

For TikTok specifically, NaijaTrendsHub uses the following official TikTok for Developers products:

  • Login Kit: To authenticate users and verify identity
  • Content Posting API: To publish videos on your behalf (with permission)
  • Display API: To retrieve basic profile information (username, follower count, etc.)

We request only the minimum scopes necessary:

  • user.info.profile – Read profile data
  • user.info.stats – Read follower/video counts

Our mission

We amplify Nigerian stories to global audiences while respecting platform guidelines, user privacy, and content authenticity.
