2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information (e.g., email address, username) if you create an account.
  • Preferences and settings you configure in the Service.
  • Support requests you send to us (e.g., via email).

  • Log data (e.g., IP address, user agent, access times) for security and diagnostics.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and secure the Service.
  • Publish content to your connected social media accounts based on your actions/settings.
  • Operate analytics to improve reliability (e.g., crash/error monitoring).
  • Communicate with you about service-related issues or support requests.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not ask for or store your social media passwords.
  • We do not access your private messages.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We may share information with:

  • Social media platforms (to publish content on your behalf and complete authorization flows).
  • Service providers (e.g., hosting and infrastructure providers) strictly as needed to run the Service.
  • Legal requirements if required by law, or to protect rights and safety.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

8. Deletion requests (no in-app delete yet)

At this time, NaijaTrendsHub does not provide an in-app “Delete account” button.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email naijatrendshub@gmail.com with the subject line DELETE MY DATA from the email address associated with your account (or include enough information for us to verify ownership).

After verification, we will delete or de-identify your personal data and delete stored platform access tokens, unless we must retain certain records for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

9. Revoking TikTok (and other platform) access

You can revoke NaijaTrendsHub’s access at any time using the platform’s settings.

  • What revocation means: Once revoked, we will no longer be able to publish to that platform account.
  • What we do after revocation: We will delete or invalidate the relevant access tokens in our system and stop scheduled publishing to that account.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies/local storage for essential functions such as maintaining sessions, preventing abuse, and basic analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

11. Third-party services

Our Service integrates with third-party platforms (e.g., TikTok, YouTube, Instagram). Their privacy policies govern their handling of data on their platforms.

12. Children’s privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

13. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, depending on infrastructure providers and platform integrations. We apply safeguards as appropriate.

14. Changes to this policy
