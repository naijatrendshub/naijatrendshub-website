Nigerian Stories, Globally Amplified

NaijaTrendsHub is an AI-powered content automation platform that helps users turn trending Nigerian news, sports, and entertainment into platform-native posts.

We publish only with your authorization after you connect your social accounts using official platform authentication (OAuth).

YouTube TikTok Instagram X (Twitter) LinkedIn Telegram Facebook Snapchat Twitch Farcaster Bluesky

How it works

  • You connect a social media account via the platform’s authorization flow.
  • NaijaTrendsHub generates original captions/scripts and prepares media for publishing.
  • You control what gets published (and can revoke access at any time).

Transparency

  • We do not ask for your social media passwords.
  • We use access tokens provided by the platform to publish on your behalf.
  • You can request deletion of your data via our Support page.
