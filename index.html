FAQ

Is my data safe? ▼
Yes. We use OAuth (no password storage), encrypt data in transit and at rest, and comply with platform security requirements. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I revoke access? ▼
Yes, anytime. Go to your platform's settings (e.g., TikTok Settings → Third-party apps) and remove NaijaTrendsHub. Visit our Support page for step-by-step instructions.
How do I delete my data? ▼
Email naijatrendshub@gmail.com with subject "DELETE MY DATA". We'll verify and delete your account within 7 business days.
What content do you publish? ▼
We publish trending Nigerian news, sports, and entertainment content. All content adheres to platform guidelines and our editorial policies (no hate speech, misinformation, or copyright violations).
